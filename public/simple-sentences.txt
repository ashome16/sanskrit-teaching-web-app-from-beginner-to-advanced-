=== प्रथमो भागः : धातवः (14 Core Roots & Verb Formation) ===
पठ् (to read) — बालकः पुस्तकं पठति। (The boy reads the book.)
लिख् (to write) — बालिका पत्रं लिखति। (The girl writes a letter.)
गम् (to go) — छात्रः विद्यालयं गच्छति। (The student goes to school.)
खाद् (to eat) — सः फलं खादति। (He eats fruit.)
पा/पिब् (to drink) — सा जलं पिबति। (She drinks water.)
धाव् (to run) — अश्वः वेगेन धावति। (The horse runs swiftly.)
क्रीड् (to play) — बालकाः क्रीडाङ्गणे क्रीडन्ति। (The boys play in the ground.)
हस् (to laugh) — शिशुः हसति। (The baby laughs.)
वद् (to speak) — अहं सत्यं वदामि। (I speak truth.)
दृश्/पश्य् (to see) — वयं मयूरं पश्यामः। (We see the peacock.)
नम् (to bow) — शिष्यः गुरुं नमति। (The student bows to the teacher.)
स्था/तिष्ठ् (to stand) — वृक्षः भूमौ तिष्ठति। (The tree stands on the earth.)
कृ (to do) — सा कार्यं करोति। (She does the work.)
भू (to be/become) — ज्ञानं श्रेष्ठं भवति। (Knowledge becomes highest.)

=== द्वितीयो भागः : 300 व्यावहारिक-वाक्यानि (300 Everyday Sanskrit Sentences) ===

--- १. शिष्टाचारः (Greetings & Etiquette) ---
1. हरिः ॐ। (Hariḥ om.) — Hello. / Greetings.
2. सुप्रभातम्। (Suprabhātam.) — Good morning.
3. शुभरात्रिः। (Śubharātriḥ.) — Good night.
4. धन्यवादः। (Dhanyavādaḥ.) — Thank you.
5. स्वागतम्। (Svāgatam.) — Welcome.
6. क्षम्यताम्। (Kṣamyatām.) — Excuse me / Forgive me.
7. कृपया। (Kṛpayā.) — Please.
8. अस्तु। (Astu.) — Okay / Let it be.
9. श्रीमन्! (Śrīman!) — Sir!
10. मान्ये! (Mānye!) — Madam!

14. भवन्तः के? (Bhavantaḥ ke?) — Who are you? (plural/respectful)
15. अहं छात्रः अस्मि। (Ahaṁ chātraḥ asmi.) — I am a student (male).
16. अहं छात्रा अस्मि। (Ahaṁ chātrā asmi.) — I am a student (female).
17. एषः मम मित्रम्। (Eṣaḥ mama mitram.) — This is my friend.

20. त्वं कुत्र असि? (Tvaṁ kutra asi?) — Where are you?

--- ५. सम्बन्धः (Possession & Family) ---

--- ६. समयः वातावरणं च (Time & Weather) ---

56. मेघः वर्षति। (Meghaḥ varṣati.) — The cloud is raining.
57. शीतलः वायुः वहति। (Śītalaḥ vāyuḥ vahati.) — Cold wind is blowing.
58. अद्य शैत्यम् अस्ति। (Adya śaityam asti.) — It is cold today.
59. सायङ्कालः अभवत्। (Sāyaṅkālaḥ abhavat.) — It has become evening.
60. कदा गमिष्यसि? (Kadā gamiṣyasi?) — When will you go?

--- ७. भोजनम् (Food & Dining) ---
61. भोजनं सिद्धं किम्? (Bhojanaṁ siddhaṁ kim?) — Is the food ready?

154. रामः लक्ष्मणः च भ्रातरौ। (Rāmaḥ lakṣmaṇaḥ ca bhrātarau.) — Rama and Lakshmana are brothers.

156. अहं गृहं गत्वा भोजनं करोमि। (Ahaṁ gṛhaṁ gatvā bhojanaṁ karomi.) — Having gone home, I will eat.
157. पुस्तकं विना पठनं न भवति। (Pustakaṁ vinā paṭhanaṁ na bhavati.) — Studying cannot happen without a book.
158. यथा राजा, तथा प्रजा। (Yathā rājā, tathā prajā.) — As the king, so the subjects.
159. सा पठितुं विद्यालयं गच्छति। (Sā paṭhituṁ vidyālayaṁ gacchati.) — She goes to school to study.
160. अहं जानामि यत् त्वं चतुरः असि। (Ahaṁ jānāmi yat tvaṁ caturaḥ asi.) — I know that you are clever.

--- १७. स्वास्थ्यम् (Health & Wellness) ---
161. मम शिरोवेदना अस्ति। (Mama śirovedanā asti.) — I have a headache.
162. भवान् औषधं स्वीकृतवान् किम्? (Bhavān auṣadhaṁ svīkṛtavān kim?) — Have you taken the medicine? (to a male)
163. तस्य नेत्रे रक्तायते। (Tasya netre raktāyate.) — His eyes are red.
164. स्वास्थ्यं परमं धनम् अस्ति। (Svāsthyaṁ paramaṁ dhanaṁ asti.) — Health is the ultimate wealth.
165. अहं क्लान्तः अस्मि। (Ahaṁ klāntaḥ asmi.) — I am tired (male).
166. सा अस्वस्था अस्ति। (Sā asvasthā asti.) — She is unwell.

168. तस्य दन्तः चलति। (Tasya dantaḥ calati.) — His tooth is loose.
169. अहं सम्यक् निद्रां कृतवान्। (Ahaṁ samyak nidrāṁ kṛtavān.) — I slept well.
170. शुद्धः वायुः आरोग्याय आवश्यकः। (Śuddhaḥ vāyuḥ ārogyāya āvaśyakaḥ.) — Clean air is necessary for health.

--- १८. प्रकृतिः पशवः च (Nature & Animals) ---
171. सिंहः वने गर्जति। (Siṁhaḥ vane garjati.) — The lion roars in the forest.
172. धेनुः दुग्धं ददाति। (Dhenuḥ dugdhaṁ dadāti.) — The cow gives milk.
173. गजः मन्दं चलति। (Gajaḥ mandaṁ calati.) — The elephant walks slowly.
174. मयूरः वर्षाकाले नृत्यति। (Mayūraḥ varṣākāle nṛtyati.) — The peacock dances in the rainy season.
175. वृक्षाः अस्मभ्यं छायां यच्छन्ति। (Vṛkṣāḥ asmabhyaṁ chāyāṁ yacchanti.) — Trees give shade to us.
176. तडागे कमलं विकसति। (Taḍāge kamalaṁ vikasati.) — The lotus blooms in the pond.
177. शशकः वेगेन धावति। (Śaśakaḥ vegena dhāvati.) — The rabbit runs fast.
178. कूर्दति वानरः वृक्षे। (Kūrdati vānaraḥ vṛkṣe.) — The monkey jumps on the tree.
179. भ्रमराः पुष्पे गुञ्जन्ति। (Bhramarāḥ puṣpe guñjanti.) — Bees buzz on the flower.
180. आकाशे तारकाः शोभन्ते। (Ākāśe tārakāḥ śobhante.) — Stars look beautiful in the sky.

--- १९. इच्छा-भूतकालः (Intentions & Past Forms) ---
181. अहं शिक्षकः भवितुम् इच्छामि। (Ahaṁ śikṣakaḥ bhavitum icchāmi.) — I want to become a teacher.
182. सः आपणं गतवान् आसीत्। (Saḥ āpaṇaṁ gatavān āsīt.) — He had gone to the shop.
183. वयं श्वः परीक्षां लेखिष्यामः। (Vayaṁ śvaḥ parīkṣāṁ lekhiṣyāmaḥ.) — We will write the exam tomorrow.
184. त्वं मम सन्देशं प्राप्तवान् किम्? (Tvaṁ mama sandeśaṁ prāptavān kim?) — Did you receive my message?

186. ते सत्यं ज्ञातवन्तः। (Te satyaṁ jñātavantaḥ.) — They came to know the truth.
187. अहं तत्र न गमिष्यामि। (Ahaṁ tatra na gamiṣyāmi.) — I will not go there.
188. माता भोजनं पचति स्म। (Mātā bhojanaṁ pacati sma.) — Mother used to cook / was cooking food.
189. बालकः रोदनं कृतवान्। (Bālakaḥ rodanaṁ kṛtavān.) — The boy cried.
190. यूयं कथं विस्मृतवन्तः? (Yūyaṁ kathaṁ vismṛtavantaḥ?) — How did you all forget?

--- २०. लघु सम्भाषणम् (Daily Conversation Idioms) ---
191. चिन्ता मा कुरु। (Cintā mā kuru.) — Do not worry.
192. सर्वं कुशलं वा? (Sarvaṁ kuśalaṁ vā?) — Is everything fine?
193. अहं शीघ्रं आगच्छामि। (Ahaṁ śīghraṁ āgacchāmi.) — I am coming quickly.
194. एषः मम अपराधः नास्ति। (Eṣaḥ mama aparādhaḥ नास्ति.) — This is not my fault.
195. भवतः साहाय्यं आवश्यकम्। (Bhavataḥ sāhāyyaṁ āvaśyakam.) — Your help is needed.
196. भवतु, नाम। (Bhavatu, nāma.) — Let it be so / Alright.
197. अहं तं न जानामि। (Ahaṁ taṁ na jānāmi.) — I do not know him.
198. एतत् सत्यं भाति। (Etat satyaṁ bhāti.) — This seems true.
199. वयम् एकत्रीभवामः। (Vayaṁ ekatrībhavāmaḥ.) — Let us gather together.
200. पुनर्मिलामः। (Punarmilāmaḥ.) — See you again / Goodbye.

--- २१. इन्द्रियानुभूतिः (Sensory Perceptions) ---
201. अहं सुगन्धं जिघ्रामि। (Ahaṁ sugandhaṁ jighrāmi.) — I smell the fragrance.
202. त्वं किं पश्यसि? (Tvaṁ kiṁ paśyasi?) — What do you see?
203. सः उच्चैः शृणोति। (Saḥ uccaiḥ śṛṇoti.) — He hears a loud sound.
204. बालकः कोमलं स्पृशति। (Bālakaḥ komalaṁ spṛśati.) — The boy touches something soft.
205. भोजनं स्वादिष्टं भासते। (Bhojanaṁ svādiṣṭaṁ bhāsate.) — The food tastes delicious.
206. सा दूरदर्शनं पश्यन्ती अस्ति। (Sā dūradarśanaṁ paśyantī asti.) — She is watching television.
207. किं त्वं कोलाहलं श्रुतवान्? (Kiṁ tvaṁ kolāhalaṁ śrutavān?) — Did you hear the noise?
208. अहं तस्य कण्ठस्वरं चिनोमि। (Ahaṁ tasya kaṇṭhasvaraṁ cinomi.) — I recognize his voice.
209. अत्र अन्धकारः वर्तते। (Atra andhakāraḥ vartate.) — It is dark here.
210. दीपं प्रज्वालयतु कृपया। (Dīpaṁ prajvālayatu kṛpayā.) — Please turn on the light.

--- २२. मानसिकभावाः (Thoughts & Feelings) ---
211. मम मनसि एकः विचारः अस्ति। (Mama manasi ekaḥ vicāraḥ asti.) — I have an idea in my mind.
212. सा अतीव प्रसन्ना दृश्यते। (Sā atīva prasannā dṛśyate.) — She looks very happy.
213. किमर्थं त्वं भीतः असि? (Kimarthaṁ tvaṁ bhītaḥ asi?) — Why are you afraid?
214. सः क्रोधेन वदति। (Saḥ krodhena vadati.) — He speaks with anger.
215. अहं भ्रान्तः अस्मि। (Ahaṁ bhrāntaḥ asmi.) — I am confused (male).
216. धैर्यं धरतु। (Dhairyaṁ dharatu.) — Have courage / Be patient.
217. मम उपरि विश्वासं कुरु। (Mama upari viśvāsaṁ kuru.) — Trust me.
218. सा दुःखं अनुभवति। (Sā duḥkhaṁ anubhavati.) — She is feeling sad.
219. अहं तव भावनां जानामि। (Ahaṁ tava bhāvanāṁ jānāmi.) — I know your feelings.
220. निराशः मा भव। (Nirāśaḥ mā bhava.) — Do not be disappointed.

--- २३. यात्रा सञ्चारः च (Travel & Commuting) ---
221. यानं शीघ्रं चलति। (Yānaṁ śīghraṁ calati.) — The vehicle moves fast.
222. अहं लोकयानेन गच्छामि। (Ahaṁ lokayānena gacchāmi.) — I am going by bus.
223. धूमशकटं विरामस्थाने अस्ति। (Dhūmaśakaṭaṁ virāmasthāne asti.) — The train is at the station.
224. त्वं कदा प्रस्थास्यसि? (Tvaṁ kadā prasthāsyasi?) — When will you depart?
225. वयं द्विचक्रिकया भ्रमामः। (Vayaṁ dvicakrikayā bhramāmaḥ.) — We ride on a bicycle/scooter.
226. मार्गः अवरुद्धः अस्ति। (Mārgaḥ avaruddhaḥ asti.) — The road is blocked.
227. अहं विमानतलं गच्छन् अस्मि। (Ahaṁ vimānatalaṁ gacchan asmi.) — I am going to the airport.
228. भवान् पत्रं प्राप्तवान् किम्? (Bhavān patraṁ prāptavān kim?) — Did you get the ticket?

230. यात्रा सुखमयी भवतु। (Yātrā sukhamayī bhavatu.) — Have a safe/happy journey.

--- २४. धनं वाणिज्यं कार्यम् (Money, Shopping & Work) ---
231. अस्य वस्तुनः मूल्यं कति? (Asya vastunaḥ mūlyaṁ kati?) — How much does this item cost?
232. मम हस्ते धनं नास्ति। (Mama haste dhanaṁ nāsti.) — I don't have money in my hand.
233. सा आपणतः शाकं क्रीणाति। (Sā āpaṇataḥ śākaṁ krīṇāti.) — She buys vegetables from the shop.
234. अहं कार्यालये कार्यं करोमि। (Ahaṁ kāryālaye kāryaṁ karomi.) — I work in an office.
235. सः नूतनं धनस्यूतं क्रीतवान्। (Saḥ nūtanaṁ dhanasyūtaṁ krītavān.) — He bought a new wallet.
236. अद्य मम अवकाशः अस्ति। (Adya mama avakāśaḥ asti.) — Today is my day off / holiday.
237. सः कुशलः तक्षकः अस्ति। (Saḥ kuśalaḥ takṣakaḥ asti.) — He is a skilled carpenter.
238. किं तव कार्यं समाप्तम्? (Kiṁ tava kāryaṁ samāptam?) — Is your work finished?
239. सा धनं सञ्चिनोति। (Sā dhanaṁ sañcinoti.) — She saves money.
240. उद्यमेन हि सिध्यन्ति कार्याणि। (Udyamena hi sidhyanti kāryāṇi.) — Works are accomplished only through hard work.

--- २५. दिशानिर्देशः (Giving Directions) ---
241. वामतः चलतु। (Vāmataḥ calatu.) — Move / turn to the left.
242. दक्षिणतः पश्यतु। (Dakṣiṇataḥ paśyatu.) — Look to the right.
243. सरलमार्गेण गच्छ। (Saraḷamārgeṇa gaccha.) — Go straight ahead.
244. मम गृहं मन्दिरस्य पार्श्वे अस्ति। (Mama gṛhaṁ mandirasya pārśve asti.) — My house is next to the temple.
245. ततः अग्रे गच्छतु। (Tataḥ agre gacchatu.) — Go further ahead from there.
246. एषः संकीर्णः मार्गः अस्ति। (Eṣaḥ saṅkīrṇaḥ mārgaḥ asti.) — This is a narrow lane.
247. उत्पीठिकायाः अधः मार्जारः अस्ति। (Utpīṭhikāyāḥ adhaḥ mārjāraḥ asti.) — There is a cat under the table.
248. गृहस्य अन्तः आगच्छ। (Gṛhasya antaḥ āgaccha.) — Come inside the house.
249. बहिः मा गच्छ, आतपः अस्ति। (Bahiḥ mā gaccha, ātapaḥ asti.) — Do not go outside, it is sunny.
250. नद्याः परतः पर्वतः वर्तते। (Nadyāḥ parataḥ parvataḥ vartate.) — There is a mountain across the river.

--- २६. यत्-तत् प्रयोगाः (Relative Clauses (यत्-तत्)) ---
251. यः पठति, सः जानाति। (Yaḥ paṭhati, saḥ jānati.) — He who studies, knows.
252. या गायति, सा मम भगिनी। (Yā gāyati, sā mama bhaginī.) — The girl who sings is my sister.
253. यत् सत्यं, तत् कटु भवति। (Yat satyaṁ, tat kaṭu bhavati.) — What is true is often bitter.
254. यदा सूर्यः उदेति, तदा कमलं विकसति। (Yadā sūryaḥ udeti, tadā kamalam vikasati.) — When the sun rises, then the lotus blooms.

259. यदा त्वं इच्छसि, तदा आगच्छ। (Yadā tvaṁ icchasi, tadā āgaccha.) — Come whenever you want.

--- २७. प्रौढ शिष्टाचारः (Advanced Etiquette) ---
261. मम अपराधं क्षमस्व। (Mama aparādhaṁ kṣamasva.) — Forgive my mistake.
262. भवतः आज्ञा शिरोधार्या। (Bhavataḥ ājñā śirodhāryā.) — Your command is accepted respectfully.
263. किञ्चित् जलेन सिञ्चतु। (Kiñcit jalena siñcatu.) — Sprinkle a little water.

279. त्वं गुरुं नमस्कृतवान् भवेः। (Tvaṁ guruṁ namaskṛtavān bhaveḥ.) — You should have bowed to the teacher.

--- २९. कालमानम् (Durations & Scheduling) ---

--- ३०. सुन्दरप्रयोगाः (Idiomatic & Concluding Expressions) ---
291. अति सर्वत्र वर्जयेत्। (Ati sarvatra varjayet.) — Excess of anything should be avoided.
292. यद् भवति, भद्राय एव भवति। (Yad bhavati, bhadrāya eva bhavati.) — Whatever happens, happens for the best.
