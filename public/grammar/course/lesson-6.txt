# Lesson 6: Feminine Nouns Ending in 'ā' and 'ī' (Ākārānta / Īkārānta Strīliṅga)

In Lesson 5, you mastered masculine nouns ending in short 'a' (Akārānta). In this lesson, we explore the feminine realm: **Strīliṅga (स्त्रीलिङ्ग)**.

The two most common feminine patterns in Sanskrit are:
1. **Ākārānta (आकारान्त):** Stems ending in long **आ (ā)** (e.g. **लता** - creeper, **बालिका** - girl, **माला** - garland).
2. **Īkārānta (ईकारान्त):** Stems ending in long **ई (ī)** (e.g. **नदी** - river, **जननी** - mother, **नारी** - woman).

---

## 1. Paradigm 1: लता (Latā - Creeper / Vine) — Ākārānta Strīliṅga

| Case (विभक्तिः) | Singular (एकवचनम्) | Dual (द्विवचनम्) | Plural (बहुवचनम्) | Everyday Meaning (Singular) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Prathamā** | **लता** (*latā*) | **लते** (*late*) | **लताः** (*latāḥ*) | The vine (Subject) |
| **2. Dvitīyā** | **लताम्** (*latām*) | **लते** (*late*) | **लताः** (*latāḥ*) | The vine (Object) |
| **3. Tṛtīyā** | **लतया** (*latayā*) | **लताभ्याम्** (*latābhyām*) | **लताभिः** (*latābhiḥ*) | By/with the vine |
| **4. Caturthī** | **लतायै** (*latāyai*) | **लताभ्याम्** (*latābhyām*) | **लताभ्यः** (*latābhyaḥ*) | For the vine |
| **5. Pañcamī** | **लतायाः** (*latāyāḥ*) | **लताभ्याम्** (*latābhyām*) | **लताभ्यः** (*latābhyaḥ*) | From the vine |
| **6. Ṣaṣṭhī** | **लतायाः** (*latāyāḥ*) | **लतयोः** (*latayoḥ*) | **लतानाम्** (*latānām*) | Of the vine |
| **7. Saptamī** | **लतायाम्** (*latāyām*) | **लतयोः** (*latayoḥ*) | **लतासु** (*latāsu*) | In/on the vine |
| **8. Sambodhana** | **हे लते!** (*he late!*) | **हे लते!** (*he late!*) | **हे लताः!** (*he latāḥ!*) | O vine! |

> **Key Rule to Avoid Common Mistakes:** Notice that Case 1 singular is **लता** (without a visarga), while plural is **लताः** (with a visarga)! In masculine nouns, Case 1 singular had a visarga (*बालकः*), but in feminine nouns ending in 'ā', the singular has NO visarga.

---

## 2. Paradigm 2: नदी (Nadī - River) — Īkārānta Strīliṅga

| Case (विभक्तिः) | Singular (एकवचनम्) | Dual (द्विवचनम्) | Plural (बहुवचनम्) | Everyday Meaning (Singular) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Prathamā** | **नदी** (*nadī*) | **नद्यौ** (*nadyau*) | **नद्यः** (*nadyaḥ*) | The river (Subject) |
| **2. Dvitīyā** | **नदीम्** (*nadīm*) | **नद्यौ** (*nadyau*) | **नदीः** (*nadīḥ*) | To the river (Object) |
| **3. Tṛtīyā** | **नद्या** (*nadyā*) | **नदीभ्याम्** (*nadībhyām*) | **नदीभिः** (*nadībhiḥ*) | By/with the river |
| **4. Caturthī** | **नद्यै** (*nadyai*) | **नदीभ्याम्** (*nadībhyām*) | **नदीभ्यः** (*nadībhyaḥ*) | For the river |
| **5. Pañcamī** | **नद्याः** (*nadyāḥ*) | **नदीभ्याम्** (*nadībhyām*) | **नदीभ्यः** (*nadībhyaḥ*) | From the river |
| **6. Ṣaṣṭhī** | **नद्याः** (*nadyāḥ*) | **नद्योः** (*nadyoḥ*) | **नदीनाम्** (*nadīnām*) | Of the river |
| **7. Saptamī** | **नद्याम्** (*nadyām*) | **नद्योः** (*nadyoḥ*) | **नदीषु** (*nadīṣu*) | In the river |
| **8. Sambodhana** | **हे नदि!** (*he nadi!*) | **हे नद्यौ!** (*he nadyau!*) | **हे नद्यः!** (*he nadyaḥ!*) | O river! |

---

## 3. Comparing Masculine and Feminine Patterns
- In Case 3 plural, feminine uses **-भिः** (*latābhiḥ*, *nadībhiḥ*), whereas masculine uses **-ैः** (*bālaka-iḥ*).
- In Cases 4 and 5 plural, feminine uses **-भ्यः** (*latābhyaḥ*, *nadībhyaḥ*), preserving the base stem.
- In Case 7 singular, feminine has the distinctive **-याम्** (*latāyām*, *nadyām*).

---

## 4. Practice Exercises
1. Decline **बालिका (girl)** in Case 1 plural: **बालिकाः** (The girls).
2. Translate: "With the mother (जननी)": **जनन्या** (Case 3 singular).
3. Translate: "In the river": **नद्याम्** (Case 7 singular).
