# Sanskrit Lesson: Understanding Vibhaktis (Case Endings)
## Master the 8 Sanskrit noun cases, kāraka roles, suffixes, and memory trick with Bālaka

In English, we use prepositions (like to, by, for, from, of, in) to show how a noun relates to a verb.
Example: The boy goes to the school.

In Sanskrit, instead of separate words, we change the ending of the noun itself. These case endings are called विभक्ति (Vibhakti). There are 8 Vibhaktis in Sanskrit, and each corresponds to a specific role in a sentence, known as a कारक (Kāraka).

Let us learn them using a standard masculine noun ending in "a": बालक (Bālaka - Boy).

## 1. प्रथमा विभक्ति (Prathamā Vibhakti) — Nominative Case
- Role (कारक): कर्ता (Kartā) — The Subject (The doer of the action).
- English Indicator: No preposition (The / A).
- Singular Suffix: -ः (-aḥ)
- Example Word: बालकः (Bālakaḥ) — The boy.
- Sentence: बालकः पठति। (Bālakaḥ paṭhati.) = The boy reads.

## 2. द्वितीया विभक्ति (Dvitīyā Vibhakti) — Accusative Case
- Role (कारक): कर्मन् (Karman) — The Direct Object (The recipient or target of the action).
- English Indicator: To / Whom / What.
- Singular Suffix: -म् (-am)
- Example Word: बालकम् (Bālakam) — To the boy (or just the boy as an object).
- Sentence: जनकः बालकम् पश्यति। (Janakaḥ bālakam paśyati.) = Father sees the boy.

## 3. तृतीया विभक्ति (Tṛtīyā Vibhakti) — Instrumental Case
- Role (कारक): करणम् (Karaṇam) — The Instrument (By means of what or with whom the action is done).
- English Indicator: By / With.
- Singular Suffix: -एण (-ena)
- Example Word: बालकेन (Bālakena) — By/With the boy.
- Sentence: शिक्षकः बालकेन सह गच्छति। (Śikṣakaḥ bālakena saha gacchati.) = The teacher goes with the boy.

## 4. चतुर्थी विभक्ति (Caturthī Vibhakti) — Dative Case
- Role (कारक): सम्प्रदानम् (Sampradānam) — The Recipient / Purpose (For whom something is given or done).
- English Indicator: For / To.
- Singular Suffix: -ाय (-āya)
- Example Word: बालकाय (Bālakāya) — For the boy.
- Sentence: माता बालकाय फलम् ददाति। (Mātā bālakāya phalam dadāti.) = Mother gives a fruit to/for the boy.

## 5. पञ्चमी विभक्ति (Pañcamī Vibhakti) — Ablative Case
- Role (कारक): अपादानम् (Apādānam) — Separation or Origin (Indicating where something moves away from).
- English Indicator: From.
- Singular Suffix: -आत् (-āt)
- Example Word: बालकात् (Bālakāt) — From the boy.
- Sentence: कन्दुकः बालकात् पतति। (Kandukaḥ bālakāt patati.) = The ball falls from the boy.

## 6. षष्ठी विभक्ति (Ṣaṣṭhī Vibhakti) — Genitive Case
- Role (कारक): सम्बन्धः (Sambandhaḥ) — Possession or Relationship (Not technically a Kāraka, but connects two nouns).
- English Indicator: Of / 's.
- Singular Suffix: -स्य (-asya)
- Example Word: बालकस्य (Bālakasya) — Of the boy / Boy's.
- Sentence: एतत् बालकस्य पुस्तकम्। (Etat bālakasya pustakam.) = This is the boy's book.

## 7. सप्तमी विभक्ति (Saptamī Vibhakti) — Locative Case
- Role (कारक): अधिकरणम् (Adhikaraṇam) — The Location or Time (Where or when the action takes place).
- English Indicator: In / On / At.
- Singular Suffix: -ए (-e)
- Example Word: बालके (Bālake) — In/On the boy.
- Sentence: बालके उत्तमः गुणः अस्ति। (Bālake uttamaḥ guṇaḥ asti.) = A good quality exists in the boy.

## 8. सम्बोधन विभक्ति (Sambodhana Vibhakti) — Vocative Case
- Role (कारक): आह्वानम् (Āhvānam) — Addressing or calling out to someone.
- English Indicator: O! / Hey!
- Singular Prefix/Suffix: हे ... ! (He ... !) (Drops the base visarga)
- Example Word: हे बालक! (He bālaka!) — O Boy!
- Sentence: हे बालक! अत्र आगच्छ। (He bālaka! atra āgaccha.) = O Boy! Come here.

By memorizing this single suffix series — -aḥ, -am, -ena, -āya, -āt, -asya, -e, He ... ! — you unlock thousands of Sanskrit masculine nouns ending in "a", including राम (Rāma), गज (Elephant), अश्व (Horse), देव (Deity), and वानर (Monkey).
