# Lesson 10: Introduction to Avyayas (Indeclinables) and Simple Dialogues

Congratulations on reaching the final lesson of the Beginner Vyākaraṇa Course! 

Up to this point, you have learned nouns and verbs that decline and conjugate. In this lesson, we introduce the secret spice of natural Sanskrit conversations: **Avyayas (अव्ययानि - Indeclinables)**.

---

## 1. What is an Avyaya?
[[figure:avyaya-unchanging]]
An **Avyaya** is a word that never changes its form. No matter what gender, case, or number surrounds it, an Avyaya remains perfectly constant:

> **सदृशं त्रिषु लिङ्गेषु सर्वासु च विभक्तिषु ।**  
> **वचनेषु च सर्वेषु यन्न व्येति तदव्ययम् ॥**  
> *(That which remains identical across all 3 genders, all 7 cases, and all 3 numbers without changing is called an Avyaya.)*

---

## 2. Everyday Avyayas You Need to Know

### A. Spatial & Question Avyayas
- **अत्र** (*atra*) = Here
- **तत्र** (*tatra*) = There
- **कुत्र** (*kutra*) = Where?
- **सर्वत्र** (*sarvatra*) = Everywhere
- **कथम्** (*katham*) = How?
- **किम्** (*kim*) = What / Why?
- **किमर्थम्** (*kimartham*) = Why / For what reason?

### B. Logical & Connecting Avyayas
- **च** (*ca*) = And  
  *(Placed after the word it connects! e.g., **रामः लक्ष्मणः च** = Rama and Lakshmana)*
- **अपि** (*api*) = Also / Even  
  *(e.g., **अहमपि गच्छामि** = I also am going)*
- **सह** (*saha*) = Together with  
  *(The noun preceding 'saha' takes Case 3! e.g., **मित्रेण सह** = with a friend)*
- **अधुना / इदानीम्** (*adhunā / idānīm*) = Now
- **सदा / सर्वदा** (*sadā / sarvadā*) = Always
- **न** (*na*) = No / Not

## 3. Real Sanskrit Dialogues (सम्भाषणम्)
Now you have all the tools to speak and understand live Sanskrit! Practice these 4 daily dialogues:

### Dialogue 1: Greeting & Introduction
- **कः:** नमो नमः! भवतः नाम किम्? (*Namo namaḥ! Bhavataḥ nāma kim?* - Greetings! What is your name?)
- **खः:** मम नाम राहुलः। भवत्याः नाम किम्? (*Mama nāma Rāhulaḥ. Bhavatyāḥ nāma kim?* - My name is Rahul. What is your name? [to a female])
- **कः:** मम नाम प्रिया। भवान् कथम् अस्ति? (*Mama nāma Priyā. Bhavān katham asti?* - My name is Priya. How are you?)
- **खः:** अहं कुशली अस्मि। धन्यवादः! (*Ahaṁ kuśalī asmi. Dhanyavādaḥ!* - I am well. Thank you!)

---

### Dialogue 2: Where are you going?
- **राहुलः:** प्रिये! त्वं कुत्र गच्छसि? (*Priye! Tvam kutra gacchasi?* - Priya! Where are you going?)
- **प्रिया:** अहम् अधुना विद्यालयं गच्छामि। (*Aham adhunā vidyālayaṁ gacchāmi.* - I am going to school now.)
- **राहुलः:** त्वया सह कः गच्छति? (*Tvayā saha kaḥ gacchati?* - Who is going with you?)
- **प्रिया:** मया सह मम मित्रम् अपि गच्छति। (*Mayā saha mama mitram api gacchati.* - My friend is also going with me.)

---

### Dialogue 3: What are you doing?
- **शिक्षकः:** बालकाः! यूयं किं कुरुथ? (*Bālakāḥ! Yūyaṁ kiṁ kurutha?* - Boys! What are you all doing?)
- **बालकाः:** आचार्य! वयं संस्कृत-पुस्तकं पठामः। (*Ācārya! Vayaṁ saṁskṛta-pustakaṁ paṭhāmaḥ.* - Respected teacher! We are reading a Sanskrit book.)
- **शिक्षकः:** उत्तमम्! (*Uttamam!* - Excellent!)
