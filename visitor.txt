# Welcome to the Board

This school does not teach Sanskrit as vowel math, memorisation grids, or hidden levels.
A letter is a place on the breath. A word lives in a sentence. After Check, you see that sentence, the word you just found is highlighted, and English names the whole sentence.

**How to play**

Pick a shelf. Tap tiles. Then Check.

1. Pick one of the seven tabs.
2. Look at the Sanskrit prompt. Tap chalkboard tiles.
3. Check. If you are right, the sentence opens. The learned word is marked. English comes last, never before Check.

English is the sentence, not a label stuck on the wrong tile. **आकाशः** is never “the sun.”

## The seven shelves

You walk left to right: a mark on the board, the throat that made it, a book in the hand, the sun in the sky, a living creature, a star, then the boy reads.

### 1. Beginners · मात्रा
Vowels do not add up. They change clothes: **आ** becomes the mark **ा** on the back of a consonant.
Tap **क** then **आ**. Check → **का बालिका पठति।** *Who is the girl reading?*

### 2. nada · नाद-पथः
The sound has a home in the mouth. Names of seats only. No treatment.
**गकारः ____** → **कण्ठस्य** → **गकारः कण्ठस्य स्थानस्य अस्ति।** *The sound ga belongs to the throat repository.*

### 3. Vastu · वस्तु
Things you can touch, and what you do with them.
**पुस्तकम्** tap **पठति** → **अहं पुस्तकं पठामि।** *I read a book.*

### 4. Naksha · नक्शा
The outer map: sky, sun, river. Not far lands.
**सूर्यः** tap **आकाशः** → **सूर्यः आकाशे अस्ति।** *The sun is in the sky.*

### 5. Nature · प्रकृतिः
Animals and plants, named as they move and stand.
**____ मन्दं चलति।** → **गजः** → **गजः मन्दं चलति।** *The elephant walks slowly.*

### 6. Sanskriti · संस्कृतिः
One heritage seed. Never print “one seed” on the tab.
**वयं सर्वे ____ धूलयः।** → **तारा** → **वयं सर्वे तारा-धूलयः।** *We are all star-dust.*

### 7. Play · क्रिया
The doer and the doing. Prompt is the doer. You tap the verb. Never write (Kartā) on a tile.
**बालकः** tap **पठति** → **बालकः पठति।** *The boy reads.*
