# The one-board manifesto

One school. One chrome. One board.
Play does the join. Sūtram watches the join. Deepakam stays the book.

---

## 1-minute loophole table

| Skin | What it actually does | Hole we walked into | Hard rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **जोडो** Glue | Sound merging. क + आ → का | Treated as a static match. If it acts like मेलः, का lies to the breath. | Tiles must morph. आ changes clothes and becomes the mark ा. |
| **मेलः** Match | Essence to category. गङ्गा → नदी. पीतः → हरिद्रा. | Direct identity (tap the target word). | Tiles stay whole. No morph. Never put the target in the tiles. |
| **रिक्तम्** Gap | Fill the blank. गकारः ____ → कण्ठस्य | Self-match on कण्ठः | The sentence with the blank is on the board. |
| **Play vs Sūtram** | Play is action. Sūtram is sight. | Sūtram cloned Beginners, or English too big | Play does the join. Sūtram traces it with **शब्दः सिध्यति**. |
| **Deepakam** | Unchanging reference book | Turning book pages into board tiles | Deepakam stays a book. The board patches the path, not the text. |

Child loop, always: **Pick a shelf. Tap tiles. Then Check.**

## The seven shelves

1. **Beginners (मात्रा)** — Entry. Tap क then आ. That physicalizes the long mark. Day 2: पीतः is turmeric; tap हरिद्रा.
2. **Body (nāda-path)** — Where the breath strikes. गकारः ____ puts the hand on the throat (कण्ठस्य). Names only. No treatment.
3. **Maths (1–10)** — Word-numbers. Build पञ्च. Not a calculator. Hold 11–20.
4. **Map (two rivers)** — Names to their fluid kind. गङ्गा → नदी. No far lands. No Hūṇa.
5. **Sanskriti (one seed)** — Heritage anchor. तारा-धूलिः, labelled BRIDGE.
6. **Play** — Active generation. Same board, as a game. The child puts the week into motion.
7. **Sūtram** — Mechanics behind the magic. Not a grammar drill. A trace of how the word succeeds: **शब्दः सिध्यति**.

Top chrome (keep): Board · Deepakam · Analyse · Vibhakti Guide.

## Check

Wrong: only “Try the row again.” No English.
Right (Beginners): tiles fuse, English is big, sentence, seed. Stays.
Right (Sūtram): TRACE is the hero — `glue("क","ा") → का` — only `a+a=ā`, `a+i=e`, `a+u=o`. English is a small caption.

जोडो glue: आ→ा, इ→ि, उ→ु, then NFC. क+आ is का, not the string कआ.
मेलः / रिक्तम् do not glue.

Yellow card: letters in `sound-toy.txt` only. Whole words: hide it. Close on shelf or new puzzle. No timer. Caption: *A picture of the rule, not a recording. Hand on throat for voice.*
