## 1-minute loophole table

| Skin | What it actually does | Hole we walked into | Hard rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **जोडो** Glue | Sound merging. क + आ → का | Treated as a static match. If it acts like मेलः, का lies to the breath. | Tiles must morph. आ changes clothes and becomes the mark ा. |
| **मेलः** Match | Essence to category. गङ्गा → नदी. पीतः → हरिद्रा. | Direct identity (tap the target word). | Tiles stay whole. No morph. Never put the target in the tiles. |
| **रिक्तम्** Gap | Fill the blank. गकारः ____ → कण्ठस्य | Self-match on कण्ठः | The sentence with the blank is on the board. |
| **Play vs Sūtram** | Play is action. Sūtram is sight. | Sūtram cloned Beginners, or English too big | Play does the join. Sūtram traces it with **शब्दः सिध्यति**. |
| **Deepakam** | Unchanging reference book | Turning book pages into board tiles | Deepakam stays a book. The board patches the path, not the text. |

Child loop, always: **Pick a shelf. Tap tiles. Then Check.**
